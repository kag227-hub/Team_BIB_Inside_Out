# STATUS — Checkpoint 1

**Team BIB · “Inside Out” Silicon Investors · Seed #13**  
**Updated October 24, 2026 — synthetic validation complete; paid pilot pending**

**What we picked and why.** We chose seed #13 to test whether LLM investor personas sell winners more often than losers. The Inside Out emotions give us proposed predictions, while character-name versus emotion-description prompts test sensitivity to recognizable character cues. The pilot has nine personas (Joy, Sadness, Fear, and Embarrassment in two arms, plus a neutral control) and three fictional scenarios expanded into paired gains/losses at 5%, 10%, and 20%. We selected **GPT-5.6 Luna** and **Claude Haiku 5.5** for a low-cost comparison across providers; settings and sources are in [MODEL_SELECTION.md](MODEL_SELECTION.md). The planned two-model pilot has **108 survey observations, 54 matched pairs, and 216 question requests** before retries.

**What we actually ran and what broke.** On October 24, the corrected EDSL 1.0.9 synthetic test produced **54 observations, 100% valid decisions, 100% valid ratings, 27 complete pairs, and zero incomplete pairs**. Every answer was deliberately fixed to hold with a rating of 4, so zero sell-rate gaps are fixtures, not behavioral findings. Paid spending was **$0**. The original test returned “Hello, world X,” making all answers invalid; the original analyzer also extrapolated synthetic cost fields as though they represented paid spending. We corrected those issues, added seeded option order, used complete pairs for the sell-rate comparison, and protected existing run artifacts. The report and validation summary are in `outputs/checkpoint1_dry_run_feasibility.md` and `outputs/checkpoint1_validation.json`. A saved prompt also confirms EDSL's default “Do not break character” system instruction, which must be disclosed when interpreting persona behavior.
